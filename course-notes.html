Course Notes and Learning Reflection

Course Overview

AE 5714905 is a core department course that strengthens systems thinking, analytical modeling, and engineering design decision-making. In this course, I am learning how technical problems can be understood, modeled, and solved using structured engineering approaches.

My focus areas include conceptual reasoning, problem framing, system-level analysis, and the connection between theoretical knowledge and practical engineering solutions.

Key Learnings

  • Understanding complex aerospace engineering systems as interconnected components.
  • Applying structured thinking to identify variables, assumptions, and constraints.
  • Framing engineering problems in a way that supports evidence-based design decisions.
  • Connecting abstract concepts with practical design and analysis workflows.
Week 1

Introduction to Systems

Learned how engineering problems should be analyzed from a system perspective instead of isolated component thinking.

Week 2

Modeling and Analysis

Studied how assumptions, parameters, and simplifications influence engineering models and final decisions.

Week 3

Problem Framing

Focused on identifying objectives, constraints, and trade-offs before selecting a design or method.

Week 4

Design Thinking

Explored how business, performance, safety, and reliability concerns influence engineering design choices.

Week 5

System Evaluation

Learned how to compare solution options based on technical feasibility, complexity, and system-level impact.

Reflection

Final Insight

This course strengthened my ability to reason across system boundaries and apply a structured engineering mindset to complex challenges.
